Members · club Grand Prix

Age-graded, so every mark counts.

Road, track and field, juniors and teams — scored against the world standard for your age and event.

Alongside the Kent leagues, Sevenoaks runs its own Grand Prix. Every race performance can count. Scores use World Masters Athletics age-graded tables, so a 17-year-old sprinter and a 70-year-old on the road can be compared fairly: your time or distance is turned into a percentage of the world standard for your age and event.

For example, a 17-year-old male sprinter runs 13.5s for 100m. The tables give the world best at that age as 10.16s, so the score is 10.16 ÷ 13.5 × 100% — 75.3%.

There are three individual competitions: a junior Grand Prix, a road Grand Prix (best six scores) and a senior track-and-field Grand Prix (best three). There is also a team Grand Prix, three athletes scoring together.

Live tables

Road is best six scores. Track and field is best three. Team is three athletes scoring together. Standings are published monthly.

Handicap

The club still runs handicap races, including the Pauline Dalton Trophy. Write-ups sit with the other race stories on Reports. Fixtures are flagged on the calendar.
