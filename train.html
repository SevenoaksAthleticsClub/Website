What to bring

Road or trail shoes are both fine, depending on the route and conditions. During the winter months we tend to stick to the paths in Knole Park or head out onto the roads around Sevenoaks, so a head torch or chest torch is essential on darker evenings. In summer, with more daylight to play with, we can explore more of Knole Park and the surrounding routes.

After the run

Whichever group you run with, we all meet back at the Sennocke Centre afterwards for a few informal stretches, to catch up with everyone else and, inevitably, finish the conversations we started somewhere out on the run!

Before you join us

Our club runs cater for a range of paces, and you won’t be left to run on your own. However, you should already be comfortable running at least 5 miles continuously.

If you’d like to give us a try, please contact us before your first session rather than simply turning up. This means we can find out a little about your running and make sure there is a leader ready to welcome you and introduce you to the most suitable group. Tuesday evening is usually the best session for your first visit.

Get in touch before you come

Where to find us
