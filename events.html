Series we race

Wear the vest and you’re racing for Sevenoaks. Filter the calendar below once you know which series you fancy.

KFL

Kent Fitness League

Winter cross country for club runners — about 5–6 miles, on Sunday mornings at parks across Kent. We host a match at Knole.

More information →
Kent League

Kent XC League

Saturday cross country through the autumn and winter, from juniors to senior men and women. No upper time limit.

More information →
Kent GP

Kent Grand Prix

A season of 10 Kent road races, from 5 miles and 10K to half marathon. Best six count for individuals; best eight rounds for the team.

More information →
Kent Champs

County Championships

Kent championships on the road, over the country and on the track. Some also count for the Grand Prix.
