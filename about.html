About the club

Around 200 members, juniors and seniors, social runners to county champions. Affiliated to England Athletics.

Who we are

Sevenoaks Athletics Club is an England Athletics affiliated club with around 200 junior and senior members. Some of us run for the company and the social side, others enjoy training and competing, and plenty do a bit of both.

A bit of history

Sevenoaks Athletics Club has existed in its present form since 1976, although organised athletics in Sevenoaks goes back considerably further. Records show that there was an athletics club in the town as early as 1885.

Road racing has also played an important part in the club’s history. The Sevenoaks Half Marathon ran from 1986 to 1993 and helped the club grow during that period. It was succeeded by the Sevenoaks 7, which continues to be held in Knole Park each July and remains an important event in the club calendar.

Our home in Sevenoaks

Our main base is Sevenoaks School, where we use the track and meet for our regular club sessions. Knole Park is also a big part of club life, providing the backdrop for weekly runs, weekend training and cross country.

Running, racing and athletics

The club takes part in a wide range of athletics throughout the year. Senior members compete in road races, cross country, track and field, both as individuals and as part of Sevenoaks AC teams.

Our road runners take part in races across Kent and further afield, including the Kent Grand Prix. During the winter, members compete in both the Kent Fitness League and Kent Cross Country League. Track and field covers everything from sprints and distance events to jumping and throwing, with competition available for senior and veteran athletes.

There’s more information about the series we race on Events.

Our junior section has its own programme of training and competition, including indoor athletics, the Kent Young Athletes League, school matches and county championships. You can find out more on our Juniors page.

Our own events

Sevenoaks AC organises events as well as taking part in them. Each summer we hold the Sevenoaks 7 in Knole Park, and during the winter we host our own round of the Kent Fitness League.

We also organise club races, handicaps and other events throughout the year. These are supported by members who volunteer their time to organise, marshal and help everything run smoothly.

Away from the running

There’s a strong community feel to Sevenoaks AC. Members share lifts to races, support each other from the sidelines and gather afterwards to debrief the race, usually with plenty of tea and cake involved.

There are organised socials too, including our last Tuesday of the month gathering at the White Hart, Christmas dinner, summer barbecue and occasional club trips. Friendships are formed through training and racing together, and the social side is very much part of club life.
