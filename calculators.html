The three tools the club uses: the SAC road calculator, Howard Grubb, and Masters Athletics.

Grand Prix scores are a percentage of the world standard for your age and event. Sevenoaks uses three calculators:

  • Club road — SAC’s own road-only tool, 2025 tables. Parkrun, 10ks, halves and the usual Grand Prix distances.
  • Howard Grubb — the full 2025 road tables, every listed distance, plus a custom km if the race is in between.
  • Masters Athletics — track and field, 2023 WMA factors (sprints, jumps, throws and walks).
