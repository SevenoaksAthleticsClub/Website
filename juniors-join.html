Joining the junior section

Our junior section is for athletes aged 9 to 17, with training divided into two groups, under 11s and over 11s.

Waiting list

Our junior section is currently very popular and we operate a waiting list for new members. Due to the number of athletes waiting for places, taster sessions are currently offered from Year 5 onwards.

If you are interested in joining, please get in touch and we can add your child to the waiting list.

Taster sessions

When a place becomes available, juniors are invited to attend three taster sessions free of charge. This gives them a chance to experience the sessions before deciding whether they would like to join the club.

After the three sessions, those wishing to join will need to complete a junior membership form and pay the appropriate membership and training fees.

Younger athletes

Our usual minimum age is 9. In exceptional circumstances, and when the junior section is not oversubscribed, younger athletes may be considered. This is usually on the condition that a parent or other responsible adult is available to assist with coaching.

A child under 9 must not be left at a training session unless their responsible adult is coaching that evening.

Collection after training

Training finishes at 19:45 and junior athletes must be collected by a responsible adult. If a parent or carer is delayed, the athlete should remain with one of the coaches until they are collected.

Safeguarding

The club’s child protection policy applies to all junior athletes under 17.

Our Welfare Officer is Paul Tabor. If you have any safeguarding or welfare concerns, you can contact Paul at paul@7oaks-ac.org.uk.
