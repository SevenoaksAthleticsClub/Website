Club bests

The best marks by a Sevenoaks athlete in each event and age group — something to chase.

Club bests are the club’s own records: the fastest road times and the best track-and-field marks, kept by age group. They sit alongside the Grand Prix. A club best is a single standout performance; Grand Prix scores add up a season.

New marks from 2025 and 2026 are listed below, split into men and women. Further down are the all-time lists — the current club best in each event and age group. Junior records live in the juniors section.

Submit a mark for the top three

Club bests must be set in a properly officiated meeting or race, and should be published on Power of 10 (exceptions are allowed for overseas ultras and similar).

The lists keep the top three performances in each event and age group. Relays keep the top performance only.

To put a candidate forward, email the details below to statto@7oaks-ac.org.uk. Use the same address if you spot an error.

  • Athlete name
  • Age group (for example W50 or U15)
  • Event (for example 800m)
  • Performance (for example 2:32.25)
  • Date
  • Venue (for example Tonbridge)
