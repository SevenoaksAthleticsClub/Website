SAC Handicap October 2023

15 October 2023

Caroline Fenwick won the October handicap by more than six minutes ahead of series leader David Hale.

Caroline was the fastest woman and David the fastest overall.

With fourth place in his fourth handicap race of the year, Jim Knight moved into second place in the series, one point behind David and one point ahead of Lucy Wilkes in third.

The next race, over five miles, starts from the track at 08:30 on Sunday 5th November.
