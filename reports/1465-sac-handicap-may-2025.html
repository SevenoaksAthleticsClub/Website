SAC Handicap May 2025

15 May 2025

Jon Maxim won the fifth race of the 2025 handicap series and was the only runner to beat his handicap in windy conditions.

Jon was also fastest on the day with Heather Fitzmaurice the fastest woman.

With just four races still to go Jon leads the series with Chris Desmond second and Tony Waldron third.

The next race, over seven miles, is on Sunday 1st June at 08:30 from the track. All are welcome.
