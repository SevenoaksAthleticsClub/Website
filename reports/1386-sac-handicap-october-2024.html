SAC Handicap October 2024

15 October 2024

Heather Fitzmaurice led from gun to tape to win the October Handicap by over two minutes from Simon Hallpike and Sam Znetyniak.

Heather was the fastest woman and James Baker the fastest overall.

Dan Witt stays in the lead in the series with Andrew Mead second, but Simon's result elevates him to third.

The next race, the penultimate event of the series, starts from the track at 08:30 on Sunday 3rd November and all are welcome.
