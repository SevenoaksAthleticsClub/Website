SAC Handicap May 2021

15 May 2021

Pauline Dalton easily won the May handicap race, the first live one since March 2020. John Denyer in second also beat his handicap. Andrew Mead was fastest overall and Pauline the fastest woman. Dan Witt continues to lead the series ahead of Graham Dwyer with Lucy Wilkes third.
