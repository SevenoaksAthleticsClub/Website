Beachy Head Marathon 2021

23 October 2021

Six Sevenoaks AC runners tackled the Beachy Head Marathon on 23 October. The race is off-road and features over 800 metres of climbing and a field of over 1500 finishers. The SAC results were:

441 Pauline Dalton W55 4:35:24
457 Sam Znetyniak W45 4:36:28
977 Rob Carr M50 5:23:28
1431 Heather Fitzmaurice W45 6:58:52
1432 James Fitzmaurice M60 6:58:53
1535 Sylvia Lewis W55 8:19:09
