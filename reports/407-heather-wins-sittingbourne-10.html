Heather wins Sittingbourne 10

26 September 2014

Heather Fitzmaurice was first female finisher at the Sittingbourne 10 on Sunday 5th October.

Heather finished twenty-second overall and fourteen seconds ahead of her nearest rival in a time of 1:10:11 over the tough course.

Heather was two places behind James McGuigan who was Sevenoaks AC's first finisher in 1:09:40, while M70 Jim Fitzmaurice also had a good run in 108th place with 1:29:10.
