Podplus Kent Spring Marathon 2021

11 April 2021

Two Sevenoaks AC runners tackled the Podplus Kent Spring Marathon on 11 April, with excellent performances.

Sally Shewell was 1st W60 in 4:11:36, while Mike Lochead was eighth overall and 3rd M40 in 3:09:22.
