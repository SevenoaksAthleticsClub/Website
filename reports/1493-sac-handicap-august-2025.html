SAC Handicap August 2025

15 August 2025

Heather Fitzmaurice won the August handicap ahead of Jon Maxim and Simon Hallpike.

Heather beat her handicap by over a minute with her best run over the seven-mile course for ten years while Jon set a new PB for the course.

With just the final race to go in the final series, Jon still leads but by just one point from Heather, with Chris Desmond another point behind in third.

The final (ever) race starts from the track at 08:30 on Sunday 7th September and all are welcome.
