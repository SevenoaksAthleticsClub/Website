SAC Juniors Support Pink Week

26 January 2017

To raise money for Breast Cancer Care during Pink Week, the Sevenoaks AC juniors ran round the sports hall for the time it took Mo Farah to win the 10,000 metres gold medal in Rio 2016 (27.05). In total they ran over 150 miles (Mo only ran just over 6 miles!). This certificate confirms that they raised a fantastic £889.50.

Congratulations to all, including Coach Nicki Thompson who organised it all and especially to the juniors for their efforts and the parents for their brilliant support for the fund-raising.
