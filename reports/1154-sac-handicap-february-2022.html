SAC Handicap February 2022

15 February 2022

Alan Male won the second race of the 2022 Sevenoaks AC monthly handicap series beating his handicap by over four minutes. He was twenty seconds clear of Keith Dowson in second place, with Lucy Wilkes in third place.

Rebecca Pickard was the fastest on the day with Graham Dwyer the fastest man.

Please note that the third race of the series, over the alternate five-mile distance, has had to be rescheduled. It will now start from the Sevenoaks School track at 08:30 on Sunday 6th March.
