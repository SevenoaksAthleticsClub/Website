SAC Handicap February 2025

15 February 2025

Sam Znetyniak won the second race of the 2025 handicap series and was fastest woman in cold and wet conditions.

Chris Desmond was second and ran the fastest time of the day.

No handicaps were beaten by a small field, although Sam was only a few seconds shy after stopping to assist a fallen runner.

The next race is on Sunday 16th March at 08:30 from the track.
