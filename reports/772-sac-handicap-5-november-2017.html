SAC Handicap 5 November 2017

5 November 2017

Neil Haggertay repeated his achievement from last month by winning the November handicap.

Andrew Milne was fastest man and Annabelle Milne the fastest woman, setting a handicap first.

Five runners are within two points at the top of the series table, and at least another dozen are in contention with just one race to go.
