Harvel 5 2022

28 May 2022

Six Sevenoaks AC athletes managed to get entries for the Harvel 5 on 28th May, with some impressive running, especially by the more senior members.

Pauline Dalton won the W55 category with Sally Shewell third, while Jim Knight was runner-up in the M70s with Lionel Stielow third.
