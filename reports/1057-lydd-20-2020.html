Lydd 20 2020

8 March 2020

With spring marathons on the horizon, nine Sevenoaks AC runners warmed up with some encouraging performances at the Lydd 20 on 8 March.

SAC's star turn on the day was Bridgit Weekes, who won the W60 category by over six minutes, while Nick Humphrey-Taylor was the club's first finisher and fifth M45 and Clare Gorman fifth W35.
