SAC Handicap March 2023

15 March 2023

Bridgit Weekes won the March handicap with a new PB over the five mile course.

Bridgit improved her women's age-category record with a score which is second best overall after over 35 years of the SAC handicap in its current format.

Sam Znetyniak was second on the day and is best placed after three races.

Bridgit was fastest woman and Chris Desmond fastest overall.

The next race over seven miles starts from the track at 08:30 on Sunday 30th April.
