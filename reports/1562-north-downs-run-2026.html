North Downs Run 2026

28 June 2026

The North Downs 30k Trail Run on 28 June 2026 was also the 7th round of the Kent Road Race Grand Prix as well as the BMAF trail championships. Eight SAC runners contested the race, with some also entering the BMAF event, with brilliant results.

Leading the Sevenoaks runners home in 27th place, Keith Dowson was 2nd M60 but importantly first from a Kent club for maximum points in the individual KGP M60 competition. SAC also had two outright category winners, with Nicola Glover 1st W35 and Bridgit Weekes 1st W65, both taking their respective BMAF titles as well as the GP points.
