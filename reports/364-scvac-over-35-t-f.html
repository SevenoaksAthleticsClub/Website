SCVAC Over-35 T&F

19 May 2014

Match 2 of the SCVAC Kent league took place on 12 May at Canterbury. We had a team of five and finished the meeting in fifth place. This is also our position in the series, having now, as predicted, overtaken Istead & Ifield.

The weather was tolerable at the outset, but then heavy rain moved in. The high jump was abandoned after the first few jumps: Fosbury flopping onto wet cushions was a most unpleasant experience! In the 200m race (M35, A string), Martin White was unlucky to be second amongst the scoring athletes with 26.9 seconds – the race had been split into two, decided on times, and the winner came from the other race with 26.8 seconds, so there was no opportunity to run head-to-head. Martin’s time was, however, a PB, perhaps facilitated by new spikes, essential in these running conditions. Maurice Marchant picked up fifth place in the M35 B race and Geoffrey Kitchener came third in 30.2 seconds in the M50 competition. These two athletes also ran over the 1500m distance, with Geoffrey coming seventh in the M35 A race, his race numbers falling off as soggy pulp on the way round, and Maurice securing fourth place in the M60s in a time of 6 minutes 31.8 seconds.

In the field events, David Simpson took third place in the M50 javelin competition with 18.46m and also fourth place in the M60 shot put. Richard Pitcairn-Knowles masqueraded as an M35 javelin thrower, securing fifth place. He also ensured that we were able to field a team in the final event, the 4x100 metre relay, in which we came fifth, undoubtedly the team with the widest age range of competitors.
