SAC Handicap August 2021

15 August 2021

Simon Hallpike won the August handicap ahead of Sylvia Lewis with Suzy Claridge in third.

Suzy continues to lead the series with Simon a point behind in second and early leader Dan Witt now five points adrift in third.

Suzy was the fastest scoring woman but newcomer Julia Davis was faster. Andy Ashlee was the fastest overall.

The next race, over five miles, starts at the Sevenoaks School track at 08:30 on Sunday 19th September.
