Tunbridge Wells Half 2015

22 February 2015

James Mason was seventh overall, Glenda Goscomb was second W55 and Chris Desmond third M50 at the Tun Wells half on 22nd February. Sixteen SAC athletes ran and Glenda only missed winning her category by not lining up further forward at the start, as she was 27 seconds faster than the W55 winner on chip times.
