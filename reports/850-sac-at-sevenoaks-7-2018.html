SAC at Sevenoaks 7 2018

13 July 2018

Nineteen senior SAC runners ran in the 25th installment of our race, with another four in the junior race. Thanks to great organisation and support, it was a very successful race and a great day, despite the heat. Freddie Naughton was 2nd in the junior race, ahead of Teddy Fitzmaurice 4th, William Hurst 8th and Maddie Fitzmaurice 12th. Heather Fitzmaurice was second woman and first W45 in the senior race, while James Mason led the SAC runners, jogging home in third place overall.
