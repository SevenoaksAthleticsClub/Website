New Year's Day Trail Run

1 January 2015

Ten SAC members made their way to Otford for the year's first trail run. Simon Lewis' (centre, with map) routes proved to be tough in the muddy conditions - but great training for the Kent fitness League. Dan led a small group on an epic 15 miler taking in Polhill Bank, Shoreham, Romney Street, Knatts Valley and back on the North Downs Way. Simon led a 7 miler (which turned out to be eight) through Shoreham, Romney Street, and Kemsing Downs. And then off to the pub for a well deserved pint.
