James Wins Kent GP Series

22 July 2015

James Graham clinched the Kent Grand Prix M60 title with M60 victory at the Bexley 10k on Sunday 12th July. This gave James six wins in the six-best-scores-out-of-ten-races competition, which means he can't be caught, even though there are still three races remaining.

James was 28th overall in the race in 40:50, with John Wallace the only other SAC runner on the day finishing 55th in 44:41.
