Larkfield 10k 2015

10 May 2015

James Graham became the M60 10k County Champion with a sub 39-minute performance at the Larkfield 10k on 10 May, beating Richard Pitcairn-Knowles' twenty-year-old M60 club record in the process. Chris Desmond was the first Sevenoaks finisher and second M50, while Sally Shewell was the third W55. New member - but old friend - Lionel Stielow paired the SAC club vest with the most famous running shorts in Kent.
