Ashford 10k 2016 - James Retains Kent GP

9 October 2016

James Graham was first M60 at the Givauden Ashford 10k on 9 October 2016, which was also the final Kent Grand Prix race of 2016. After his M60 win the previous weekend at the Tonbridge Half, James finished the series with an almost-perfect 59 points from his six best GP races to retain the title he won last year.

In another repeat performance, Sally Shewell was first W55 at Ashford and will retain her Kent GP second place from 2015 when the final results are consolidated by KCAA.

SAC's top performer in the 2016 GP series, Dan Witt, also ran well on the day and should be well up in the M40 category as well as the overall positions when the Tonbridge and Ashford outcomes are digested.
