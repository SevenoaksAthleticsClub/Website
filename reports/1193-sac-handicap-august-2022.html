SAC Handicap August 2022

15 August 2022

Andrew Mead won the August handicap ahead of Suzy Claridge.

Andrew was fastest overall and Suzy was fastest woman.

Bridgit Weekes was third and Suzy now leads the series after eight of the twelve races ahead of Lucy Wilkes with Bridg third.

The next race is over five miles starting at 08:30 from Sevenoaks School track on Sunday 18th September.
