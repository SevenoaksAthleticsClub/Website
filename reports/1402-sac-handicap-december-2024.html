SAC Handicap December 2024

8 December 2024

Dan Witt made sure of winning the 2024 handicap trophy with an emphatic victory in the final race of the series over seven hilly and stormy miles on 8 December.

John Denyer was second in the race and Sam Znetyniak third while Dan was the fastest and Sam the fastest woman.

Neither Simon Rowe, second in the series, nor Andrew Mead, third, contested the final race allowing Dan to finish three points clear at the top. Simon and Andrew finished tied on points with Simon taking second in a tight tiebreak.

The next race is the first of the 2025 series and starts from the Sevenoaks School track at 08:30 on Sunday 19th January.
