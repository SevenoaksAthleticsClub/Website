SAC Handicap January 2023

15 January 2023

Sam Znetyniak won the first club handicap race of 2023 ahead of Lucy Wilkes.

Sam was the only runner to beat her handicap over the five mile course.

Chris Desmond in third was the fastest on the day with guest Francesca Desmond the fastest woman and Lucy the fastest scoring woman.

The next race, over the seven mile course, is on Sunday 26th February at 08:30 from the track.
