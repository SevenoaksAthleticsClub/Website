SAC at KXCL 2025-26 Footscray Meadows

11 October 2025

In warm temperatures, bright sunshine and a firm but lumpy course, six senior SAC runners took part in the first Kent League event of the new cross-country season at Footscray Meadows on 11 October, with three in each of the 5k and 8k races.

John Denyer was first M75 in the 5k event while, in the longer event,Yon Marsh was 2nd M65 and Oloff van Zyl 2nd M55.

The SAC results were as follows:
