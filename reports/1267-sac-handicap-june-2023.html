SAC Handicap June 2023

15 June 2023

Keith Dowson won the June handicap race ahead of David Hale and Rebecca Pickard.

Those three runners beat their handicaps over seven miles in fine conditions.

Rebecca was the fastest woman and David the fastest overall.

Lucy Wilkes increased her lead in the series by one point after coming fourth in the race. Sam Znetyniak and Simon Hallpike stay in second and third.

The next, seventh, race is over the five mile course and starts from the track at 08:30 on Sunday 16th July.
