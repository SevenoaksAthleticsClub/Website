Weald 10k 2015

6 September 2015

David Lee was second overall as fifteen SAC runners tackled the hilly Weald St Georges 10k on Sunday 6th September. The SAC turnout for the race was lower than usual due to holidays, injuries and other races, but there were still some strong performances with Lee Lintern also in the top ten and Jim Knight completing his first 10k since his second hip operation. Sophie Lamb was our first woman.
