Kent Masters XC Champs 2022

3 December 2022

Laura Bestow was 7th W35 and Rebecca Pickard 9th W45 as just five SAC runners contested the county veterans championships at Dartford's Central Park. In the biggest field of the day, Oloff Van Zyl was 10th M50 and Chris Desmond 40th. Track and field specialist Geoff Kitchener was SAC's oldest competitor finishing 12th in the M70s.
