KFL Knole Park - 30 October 2016

30 October 2016

There was a huge turnout of 561 runners for the opening KFL fixture of the 2016/17 season on 30 October in Knole Park, including a large contingent of 42 from the home club. There were some excellent performances with Rob Carr and his SAC helpers once again putting on a great race.
