KFL 2014/15 Match 5 - Nurstead Court

28 December 2014

Twenty SAC runners contested the match at Nurstead Court, Meopham on 28 December with the women only just quorate. However all three of them ran well led by Cath Linney who was sixth overall and third W45. Chris Desmond led the men to third place in the team stakes as he was first M50 (and also first M45). Sally Shewell was third W55 in a tight contest in which only three seconds separated the top three.
