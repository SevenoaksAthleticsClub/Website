KXCL 2019/20 Match 4 - Footscray

14 December 2019

Sevenoaks AC once again fielded a full senior men's team in the Kent XC League as James Graham (170th, 49:48) joined ever-presents Andrew Mead (98th (10th over-50), 43:42), Dan Witt (128th, 45:21) and Zachary Ramsden (145th, 47:11) to finish 16th on the day at the match on 14 December at Footscray. The men stand 14th after three matches.

No senior women ran at Footscray, but Suzy Claridge completed her third match this season (53rd (11th W45), 22:36) at Danson Park on 9 November.

The Kent League results are here.

The final match for men and women is at Norman Park, Bromley on 8th February. SAC seniors/veterans can contact to run in this race.
