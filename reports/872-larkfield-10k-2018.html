Larkfield 10k 2018

16 September 2018

Thirteen Sevenoaks runners tackled the Larkfield 10k on 16 September which was also the Kent County Athletics Association short course championship road race. John Denyer was second M70 and led the SAC men's 60+ team, with Simon Hallpike and Lionel Stielow, to county championship bronze. Bridgit Weekes and Sally Shewell were third and fourth W55s and, in his first race for a while, David Ives was first SAC finisher and led our M40 team, with Nick Humphrey-Taylor and Graham Dwyer, to county championship silver.
