Tonbridge 10k 2016

23 October 2016

Alastair Moffatt was fourth overall and first M40 at the Tonbridge 10k at Hildenborough on Sunday 23rd October. Other Sevenoaks runners also did well in their categories, with Anna Humphrey-Taylor third W45, Nick Humphrey-Taylor third M40, Tony Horlock fifth M50 and Simon Hallpike second M60.
