Kent XC League 2015/16 Final Match - Foots Cray Meadows

13 February 2016

After winning the M60 category in the first three races of the Kent Cross Country League, James Graham succumbed to injury. But he needed a decent result in the final race of the series at Foots Cray Meadows on February 13th in order to win the category.

Together with SAC clubmates and fellow Kent League frequenters Chris Desmond and Dan Witt, James contested the final race in which Dan Witt finished 63rd, Chris Desmond 64th, and James Graham (while still recovering from injury) 99th. More importantly, James was 4th in the M60s, clinching the overall M60 title by five points.

In the overall men's category, our runners were tightly packed with Dan 14th, Chris 15th (5th M50), and James Graham
16th (1st M60).
