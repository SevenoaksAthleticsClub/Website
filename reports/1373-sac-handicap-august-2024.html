SAC Handicap August 2024

15 August 2024

Chris Desmond won the August handicap (on 21 July), from Simon Hallpike and James Baker.

James was the fastest on the day with fourth-placed Sam Znetyniak the fastest woman.

After eight races, Andrew Mead continues to lead the series ahead of Simon Rowe, with Simon Hallpike getting closer on points in third place.

The next race, over five miles, starts from the track at 08:30 on Sunday 8th September and all are welcome.
