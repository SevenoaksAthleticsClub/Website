SAC Handicap June 2024

15 June 2024

Nicola Glover won the June handicap and ran the fastest time.

In a small field, Simon Hallpike was second and Andrew Mead third.

That puts Andrew in the lead in the series at halfway, ahead of Simon Rowe and David Hale.
