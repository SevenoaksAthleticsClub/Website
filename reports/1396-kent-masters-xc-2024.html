Kent Masters XC 2024

30 November 2024

Injury and illness depleted SAC's attendance at the Kent Masters Cross Country Championships at Central Park, Dartford on 30th November, leaving only five contestants in the men's races, and none in the women's.

Oloff Van Zyl was Sevenoaks' star performer on the day with sixth place in the M50 event, the first race, over about 8k.

The other four runners were all in the second race over 5k with Keith Dowson 13th and Chris Desmond 28th in the M60s and Simon Hallpike and Geoffrey Kitchener 10th and 19th in the M70s.
