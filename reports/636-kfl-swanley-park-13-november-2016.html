KFL Swanley Park - 13 November 2016

13 November 2016

The SAC men's and women's teams were both third and the combined team second as thirty-two members tackled the 5+ miles course on a fine autumn morning at Swanley Park. After this second KFL match of the series, the men stand in fourth position, the women third and the combined team third. James Graham was first M60 and Sally Shewell second W55, while Cath Linney led the women and Darrell Smith the men.

Sevenoaks AC was also very well represented in the junior race, as reported by Darrell Smith:

Eight of the Junior Athletic teams turned to compete. In the U11 Girls race, Alice Lampugh came in 3rd in her first race for the Club followed by Ella Baker in 7th and Tabitha Elcoch-Haskins in 11th. In the U11 boys race Verner Krynauw and Ollie Armes finished well in 2nd and 3rd, reversing the order from the Knole race. Both boys are having a great season. And in his first race for the Club, Jamie Christopher Clarke finished well in 11th to bring the team home to prove Sevenoaks AC as the strongest team of 3.

In the U14 Girls, Julia Sackville-West was third, while in the U14 Boys, Harvey Taylor was eighth.

Well done Junior SAC team!
