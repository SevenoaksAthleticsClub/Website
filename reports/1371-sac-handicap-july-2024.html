SAC Handicap July 2024

21 July 2024

John Denyer won the July handicap race by three minutes fom Dan Witt with Chris Desmond third.

David Hale was the fastest runner overall and Sam Znetyniak was the fastest woman.

After seven races, Andrew Mead maintains a clear lead in the series ahead of Simon Rowe with Simon Hallpike moving up to third place but some way behind.

The next (August) race is this coming Sunday 21st July as the track is closed throughout August. It starts from the Sevenoaks School track at 08:30 and all are invited.
