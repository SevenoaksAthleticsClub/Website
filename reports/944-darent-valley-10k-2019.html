Darent Valley 10k 2019

12 May 2019

Fifteen Sevenoaks AC runners took part in the Kent Grand Prix Darent Valley 10k race in near-perfect conditions on 12 May. Sally Shewell was third W55, while Andrew Milne led the SAC contingent home in under 40 minutes despite the hilly - but scenic - course.
