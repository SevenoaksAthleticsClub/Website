SAC Handicap December 2021

15 December 2021

Sam Znetyniak chalked up her third successive win in the handicap series, taking the final event by nearly two minutes. But with only three scoring runs, Sam made no effect on the series placings.

Suzy Claridge had already won the series, but she finished on a high smashing her nineteen-year-old PB over the seven mile course. Suzy was third on the day, behind Jane Morgan, and second-fastest overall behind Andrew Mead.

Lucy Wilkes' fourth place in the race made sure of third in the series, while Simon Hallpike remained in second.

Now that the 2021 series is completed, the first race of the 2022 series is next up. It starts from the Sevenoaks School track at 08:30 on Sunday 9th January.
