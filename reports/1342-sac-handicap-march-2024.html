SAC Handicap March 2024

15 March 2024

David Killpack (right) won the March handicap ahead of Dan Witt (left), with the first six runners beating their handicaps in wet and sometimes slippery conditions. (Note the flyers they're holding to publicise our race on 7 July.)

Suzy Claridge was the fastest woman and David Hale fastest overall.

David K was the third different winner as no-one has yet emerged as a strong challenger for the series.
