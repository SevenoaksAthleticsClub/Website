Knole Park Relay - June 11th

11 June 2013

KNOLE HOUSE RELAY – Tue 11 June 2013 – RESULTS

Congratulations to Clair Thornton and newcomers Judy Howells and Mike Fanning on their victory.

The results include the individual lap times for everyone. Below the team and individual results you will find a fastest lap ranking headed by Mike Fanning, closely followed by Gabe Morris, and below that a ranking of pace consistency across the 3 laps calculated as a % variation in lap time from the first lap. This ranking is headed jointly by Emily Faulkner and Jon Copping who both ran all 3 of their laps in exactly the same time - the odds against that must be very long!
