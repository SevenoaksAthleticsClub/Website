SAC Handicap May 2023

15 May 2023

Jim Knight won the May handicap race ahead of David Hale and Keith Dowson.

Five runners beat their handicaps over five miles in fine conditions.

Lucy Wilkes was the fastest woman and David the fastest overall.

After five races, Lucy leads the series from Sam Znetyniak and Simon Hallpike.

The next, sixth, race is over the seven mile course and starts from the track at 08:30 on Sunday 4th June.
