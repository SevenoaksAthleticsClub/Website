KFL 2022/23 Match 5 - Betteshanger Park

15 January 2023

No fewer than 27 SAC runners made the trip to Deal for the fifth match of the Kent Fitness League 2022/23 season at Betteshanger Park on 15th January.

Both the women's and men's teams ran well with the women 2nd, men 3rd and the combined team 2nd.

Scorers for the women were Bridgit Weekes, Caroline Fenwick, Andrea Berquez and Suzy Claridge, while Jeremy Winter, William Palmer, Sean Leith, Andrew Hutchinson, Ed Saunders, James Mason, Javier Montoya Montero and Mike Lochead scored for the men.
