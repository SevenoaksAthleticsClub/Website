Paddock Wood Half 2015

29 March 2015

Ron Denney was second M70 and Glenda Goscomb third W55 as fourteen Sevenoaks AC runners tackled the windswept Paddock Wood Half Marathon on Sunday 29th March. Joe Parsons led the SAC runners home with a splendid PB 1:24, Dan Witt and Tex Ryan also got PBs and John Stevens ran his second 1:27 in eight days.
