Club AGM 2014

11 April 2014

Highlights of the well-attended Annual General Meeting on 11 April included confirmation that membership fees would stay at the current level for another year. The attendees also voted for a lower fee to second claim members already affiliated to EA. There was also agreement to donate half the profits from the Sevenoaks 7 to charity.

The activity reports included the award of trophies, including the Grand Prix trophy for Pauline Dalton and the athletic achievement award for Chris Desmond.
