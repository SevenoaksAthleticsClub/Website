KXCL 2021-22 Match 2 - Somerhill

23 October 2021

Sevenoaks AC once again closed out teams in the senior men's and under-15 boys categories at the second KXCL match of the series at Somerhill on 23rd October. Again there were brilliant individual performances in the women's categories where Andrea Berquez was first masters athlete. Andrea also leads the masters category in the series (after two races) while Holly Diprose is 3rd u15g, Oloff Van Zyl 4th M50, the senior men's team 12th and the u15b team 6th.
