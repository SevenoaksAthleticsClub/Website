Canterbury 10 2015

25 January 2015

James Graham became M60 county 10 mile champion at the Canterbury 10 on Sunday 25 January. The 61-year-old also displaced John Denyer from second on the all-time club bests list by five seconds with his 66:34 chip time.

Dan Witt led the seven Sevenoaks AC runners home in bright and slightly warm conditions.
