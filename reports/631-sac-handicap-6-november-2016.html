SAC Handicap 6 November 2016

6 November 2016

Darius Sarshar won the penultimate handicap race of 2016, while Geoff Vine bowed out in style with second place in his last SAC handicap race.

Grace Manzotti still leads the series but now by only one point from John Copping. There are three in the race for the series trophy and another three with chances for the minor placings at the final race on Boxing Day, 26th December. The details are here.
