SAC Handicap March 2025

15 March 2025

Jon Maxim won the March (five-mile) handicap race ahead of Anthony Waldron and Heather Fitzmaurice.

Jon was the fastest man and Heather the fastest scoring woman, but Jane Waldron, in her first SAC handicap race, was fastest overall on the day.

After three races, no-one has yet emerged as a likely challenger for the 2025 series.

The fourth race, over the seven mile course, starts from the track at 08:30 on Sunday 6th April and all are welcome.
