Eridge 10 2015

13 September 2015

Four Sevenoaks AC runners tackled the Eridge 10 on 13 September, including Dan Witt, who reported: "As you'd expect from this race it was really muddy and wet challenge, a good warm up for the upcoming XC season. With the added bonus of a pint of Larkins 3.4% abv as a reward for all finishers!"

Dan was 29th in 01:20:55, followed by Clair Thornton 244th in 01:46:18, Jane Morgan 246th in 01:46:28, and Simon Lewis 295th in 01:54:15. The full results are here.
