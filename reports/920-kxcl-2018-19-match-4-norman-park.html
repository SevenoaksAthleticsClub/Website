KXCL 2018/19 Match 4 - Norman Park

9 February 2019

The last KXCL race of the season was held on Saturday 9th February at Norman Park on what turned out to be a surprisingly good course, writes James Graham. Unfortunately we only had two club members taking part, mainly due to fatigue and injuries.

In the senior/vets race Allan Lee had an excellent run finishing as 2nd M40 (1st M45) and in the M70 race RIchard Pitcairn-Knowles completed the race and, in doing so, finished the season in fourth place in the M70 category. Both were eighth in their races on the day. The full results are here.
