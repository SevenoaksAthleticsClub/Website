SAC Handicap September 2023

15 September 2023

David Hale won the September handicap in hot, humid conditions ahead of Jim Knight with Lucy Wilkes third.

David was the fastest scorer overall, with Lucy fastest scoring woman, although newcomer Nicolene Hanekom matched David's time. Will Witt made his first appearance for 31 years.

David now leads the series by two points, with Lucy second and Sam Znetyniak still third.

The next race, over seven miles, starts from the track at 08:30 on Sunday 8th October.
