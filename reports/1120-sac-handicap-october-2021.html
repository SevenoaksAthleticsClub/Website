SAC Handicap October 2021

21 October 2021

Sam Znetyniak won the October handicap by over four minutes, with Rebecca Pickard second and Suzy Claridge third.

Rebecca was fastest woman and Dan Witt fastest overall.

Suzy still leads the series ahead of Simon Hallpike, with Dan third on the same points as Bridgit Weekes and Lucy Wilkes.

With two races to go, Suzy can be sure of the trophy with one more win.

The next race is on Sunday 21st October.
