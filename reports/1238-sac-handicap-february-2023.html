SAC Handicap February 2023

26 February 2023

Lucy Wilkes won the second race of the 2023 handicap series on 26 February by two and a half minutes with a big new PB over the seven mile course.

Rebecca Pickard was the fastest woman and Chris Desmond was fastest overall as the two tied for fourth place.

The third race comes the following Sunday on 5th March, starting at 08:30 from the track. It is over the alternate five mile course and newcomers are very welcome.
