Larkfield 10k 2023

17 September 2023

Ten SAC runners contested the Larkfield 10k on 17th September and came away with five medals after some great performances.

In a race that doubled as the Kent Road Race Short-Course Championships, Paul Fanti led the team home in twentieth place overall and was 2nd M50.

The other medallists were Caroline Fenwick, 3rd W45, Dawn Nunes, 3rd W35, Bridgit Weekes, 1st W65 and Simon Hallpike, 1st M70.
