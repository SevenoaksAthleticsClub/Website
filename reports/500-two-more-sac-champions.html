Two more SAC champions

20 August 2015

Two Sevenoaks AC athletes won gold at the Veterans AC 10,000 metres track championships on 2nd September. John Denyer (4th from right) won the B race and the M65 title in 42:21, and Richard Pitcairn-Knowles (4th from left} won the M80 title in 63:05. The full results are here.
