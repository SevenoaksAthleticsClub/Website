SAC (Virtual) Handicap March 2021

15 March 2021

After winning the previous race, Bridgit Weekes won again in the third race of the 2021 handicap series, this time with a record WMA score.

Suzy Claridge was the fastest woman, finishing second, just two seconds behind Bridgit.

Dan Witt in fourth was fastest overall.

The next race is over seven miles.
