SAC Handicap March 2022

15 March 2022

Keith Dowson won the March handicap by twelve seconds ahead of Jane Morgan, with Chris Desmond third.

Andrew Mead was the fastest overall, with Suzy Claridge the fastest woman.

After second in the previous race, Keith is the early favourite for the series title.

The next race is on Sunday 24th April starting from the Sevenoaks School track at 08:30.
