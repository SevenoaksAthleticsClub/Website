Paddock Wood Half 2022

13 March 2022

Seven SAC runners completed the Paddock Wood Half Marathon in excellent running conditions on 13th March. John Stevens led the Sevenoaks runners home and was fourth M60 in a high quality field of nearly 1300 finishers.
