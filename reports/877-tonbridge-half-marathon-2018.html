Tonbridge Half Marathon 2018

7 October 2018

By finishing second W55 from a Kent club at the Tonbridge Half on 7th October, Sally Shewell secured the 2018 Kent AA Road Running Grand Prix title in her category after finishing runner-up in 2015 and 2016. With no others in catching distance, Sally was level on points with Invicta EK runner Vicky Talbot-Rosner as the race, the tenth and final one in the series, started. But Sally's excellent run and Talbot-Rosner's no-show means that Sally is the clear KGP winner.
