High Elms 10k 2025

8 June 2025

Only a small contingent of SAC runners contested the sixth race of the Kent Grand Prix series, the hilly trail High Elms 10k on 8th June. But with decent returns as Heather Fitzmaurice was second W55 in 52:18 and Simon Hallpike first M70 in 55:30. They were led home by Andrew Mead, 46th overall in 45:43.

The next race in the series is is the North Downs 30k on 29th June, followed by the big one - the Sevenoaks 7- on13th July. Details of the series are here, while the full results from High Elms are here.
