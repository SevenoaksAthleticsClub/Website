Sevenoaks 7 Donation to Dame Kelly Holmes Trust

29 April 2016

Race Director Darrell Smith presented the Dame Kelly Holmes Trust charity with a cheque from the proceeds of the 2016 Sevenoaks Seven race at Dame Kelly's cafe in Hildenborough on Saturday 29th April. Members had gathered ahead of a club trail run that morning.

Entries are already being taken for this year's Sevenoaks Seven race on bank holiday Monday 28th August, full details of which are here.
