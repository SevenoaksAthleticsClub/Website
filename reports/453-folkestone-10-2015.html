Folkestone 10 2015

3 April 2015

James Graham was first M60 and Sally Shewell third W55 as five Sevenoaks AC athletes contested the Folkestone 10 on Good Friday 3rd April 2015. Chris Desmond led the SAC runners home in the Kent Grand Prix event.
