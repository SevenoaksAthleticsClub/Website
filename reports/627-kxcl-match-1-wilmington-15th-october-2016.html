KXCL Match 1 Wilmington 15th October 2016

15 October 2016

Eight Sevenoaks AC athletes contested the first Cross Country race of the season: the Kent League match at Stanhill Farm, Wilmington on Saturday 15th October. Andrew Mead led home a complete men's team which finished 12th on the day and in which James Graham finished first M60 in a strong field:

78 00:39:16 ANDREW MEAD S'oaks AC M V45 770
91 00:39:54 DAN WITT S'oaks AC M V40 774
96 00:40:04 JAMES GRAHAM S'oaks AC M V60 773
147 00:43:02 CHRIS DESMOND S'oaks AC M V50 775
223 00:56:22 FRANK MASON S'oaks AC M V55 771

SAC also had two representatives in the senior women's race, with Jacqui O'Reilly our first in and Sally Shewell finishing second W55:

27 00:22:20 JACQUELINE O'REILLY S'oaks AC F Senior 779
62 00:24:42 SALLY SHEWELL S'oaks AC F V55 778

And we also fielded the only M80 competing on the day, as Richard Pitcairn-Knowles finished 5th in the M70 race:
