KXCL 2017/18 Match 3 - Footscray Meadows

25 November 2017

SAC fielded a scoring team in the senior men's race at the third match of the Kent County cross country league at Footscray Meadows on Saturday 11th November. Darius Sarchar was our first finisher in 112th, followed by Andrew Mead in 131st, Dan Witt in 151st and James Graham in 174th (3rd M60). The team was 18th on the day, maintaining their 15th position out of 24 clubs after three races.

There was no senior women's race, but Ollie Brownridge had another great run in the U13 boys, finishing 11th. The full results are here.

Ollie Brownridge is also the club's star performer in the league positions to date, standing 10th in his category. The individual tables are here. There are also team tables here.

The next fixture is on Saturday 25th November 2017 at Danson Park, Bexleyheath. This is the final match for junior categories, but there is no senior men's race. The final match, at which there are no junior races, will be at Sparrows Den, West Wickham on Saturday 10th February 2018.
