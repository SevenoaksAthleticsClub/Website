SAC Handicap April 2023

15 April 2023

Simon Hallpike won the April handicap race by 28 seconds from Graham Dwyer with Lucy Wilkes first woman 2 seconds further back in third.

Rebecca Pickard was fastest woman and Graham was the fastest overall.

In the first series table of 2023 Simon leads with Chris Desmond second.

The next race, over five miles, is on Sunday 28th May at 08:30 from the track.
