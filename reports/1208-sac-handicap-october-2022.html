SAC Handicap October 2022

15 October 2022

Andrew Mead won the October handicap race with his fastest time over the seven mile course for ten years.

James Baker was second, just ahead of Simon Hallpike.

Suzy Claridge was the fastest woman and Andrew the fastest overall.

Andrew now leads the series by four points ahead of Suzy, with two races to go and another five runners in close pursuit.

The next race, over five miles, is on Sunday 6th November at 08:30 from the track.
