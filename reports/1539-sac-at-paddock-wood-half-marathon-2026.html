SAC at Paddock Wood Half Marathon 2026

8 March 2026

Near perfect conditions - cloudy, nine degrees C and nearly windless - helped SAC runners set some great times at the 2026 Paddock Wood Half on 8 March.

With plenty of competition from 2447 finishers at the sold-out event, Nicolene Hanekom led the SAC runners home in 1:22:13 for 7th woman and 2nd W45. She was closely followed by Keith Dowson demolishing his M60 club best of 1:25:58 with 1:22:48 for 2nd M60.
