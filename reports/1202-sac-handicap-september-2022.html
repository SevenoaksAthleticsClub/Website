SAC Handicap September 2022

15 September 2022

Andrew Mead won the September handicap by one second from James Baker.

Andrew ran the fastest five-mile time of the series so far, while Suzy Claridge was the fastest woman on the day.

Bridgit Weekes broke her own women's WMA record for the handicap with a score of over 80%.

Suzy still leads the series, but four runners are now tied two points behind her with three races to go.

The next race is a seven-miler on Sunday 2nd October at 08:30 from the track.
