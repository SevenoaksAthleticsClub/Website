Keith is Marathon Champion

10 September 2014

Keith Dowson

was first M50 and third overall in the Kent County Marathon Championships race at Thanet on 7 September. Only a few seconds behind second place, Keith finished in 2:59:13. The race was also the BMAF championships, making Keith the reigning British M50 marathon champion.

Also in the Full marathon, Rob Carr was 173rd in 5:09:40, and Maryanne Aitken was 191st in 5:36:53. Scott Haggarty was 93rd in the accompanying Half Marathon in 1:53:31.
