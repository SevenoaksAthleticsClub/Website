Valentines 10 2019

17 February 2019

While eighteen other SAC runners were contesting the Tunbridge Wells half on 17 February, three of our athletes took part in the tough Valentines 10 miler at Vigo, near Meopham, where Marion van Lille had a brilliant run to finish second woman (1st senior).

Andy Ashlee also had a good run to finish seventh overall and second M40, with Maria Ashlee making up the Sevenoaks contingent.
