SAC Handicap November 2024

15 November 2024

Simon Rowe won the November handicap to move to second place in the series. Heather Fitzmaurice was second with Sam Znetyniak third.

Heather was the fastest woman and Chris Desmond fastest overall.

Simon is now two points behind Dan Witt and ahead of Andrew Mead on tiebreak. Those three are the only runners who can now win the series trophy.

All will be decided at the final race on Sunday 8th December, starting at 08:30 from the track.
