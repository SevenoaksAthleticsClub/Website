SAC Handicap January 2022

15 January 2022

Tony Horlock won the January handicap race by twenty-nine seconds, ahead of John Denyer.

Andrew Mead in third place was fastest overall, with Lucy Wilkes in fifth the fastest woman.

Winner of the previous three races, Sam Znetyniak, was fourth.

The second race of the 2022 series is next up. It starts from the Sevenoaks School track at 08:30 on Sunday 27th February.
