Larkfield 10k 2016

8 May 2016

James Graham was once again the star as eleven SAC runners completed the Larkfield 10k on 8 May in debilitatingly hot conditions. James was 16th overall and first M60 by nearly eight minutes as he led the SAC team home in under 40 minutes.
