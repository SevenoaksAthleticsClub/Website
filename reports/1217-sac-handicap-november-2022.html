SAC Handicap November 2022

15 November 2022

Jane Morgan won the penultimate race of the 2022 handicap series in torrential rain.

Lucy Wilkes was second and Sam Znetyniak third as women dominated this eleventh race of the series.

Suzy Claridge was fastest with Chris Desmond the fastest man.

Andrew Mead missed the race but continues to lead the series, with Jane now in second place and the only runner who can catch him.

All will be resolved in the final event on Sunday 11th December at 08:30 at the track.
