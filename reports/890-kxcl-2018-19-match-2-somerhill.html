KXCL 2018/19 Match 2 - Somerhill

27 October 2018

Three club members took part in the second KXCL match at Somerhill on 27 October, advises James Graham: Richard Pitcairn-Knowles (11th M70 on day, 6th after 2 matches, 1st M85), Chris Desmond (145th on day, 100th after 2 matches, 20th M50 on day, 17th M50 after 2 matches, 9th M55), and Dan Witt (154th on day, 59th M40). And we also had a ‘guest’ runner, Darrell Smith, who hadn’t registered. The full results are here.
