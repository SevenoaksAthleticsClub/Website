SAC Handicap July 2025

15 July 2025

Heather Fitzmaurice easily won the July handicap ahead of Simon Hallpike and Sam Znetyniak.

Heather beat her handicap by a minute and was the fastest runner on the day, as well as the best age-related performer.

With just two more races to go in what's likely to be the final series in the current format, Jon Maxim still leads by two points ahead of Chris Desmond, with Heather moving into third place another point behind and ahead of Simon Hallpike on tiebreak.

The penultimate race of the series, and the final one over the alternate seven-mile course, starts from the track at 08:30 on Sunday 17th August and all are welcome.
